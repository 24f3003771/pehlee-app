## 👑 The 8-Screen Awadhi Design System

The application now features the exact visual identity and 8-screen mobile flow from the product design spec:

1. **Screen 1: Your content. Your choice.**  
   *Basic Reel (₹999)*, *Standard Reel (Most Popular - ₹1,999)* in royal crimson with gold border, and *Premium Reel (₹3,999)*.
2. **Screen 2: Pick your preferred time.**  
   Weekday date selector, golden *TODAY Nov. 20* badge, and ivory time-slot selector grid (`02:30 PM` active).
3. **Screen 3: Finding your creator...**  
   Concentric glowing gold radar pulse, nearby Lucknow creator pins with checkmark seals, and a center glowing circle with the **30s SLA countdown**.
4. **Screen 4: Your creator is confirmed!**  
   Celebration confetti 🎉, floating ivory card with **Rohan Mehta (★ 4.9, 🏅 Gold Verified)**, and the large green **✔ Confirmed!** button.
5. **Screen 5: Track every step.**  
   Vertical step tracker on ivory card with **3. Shoot in progress** highlighted in a glowing amber/gold capsule pill.
6. **Screen 6: Your reel is ready!**  
   9:16 vertical cafe reel player with play overlay, **Download** & **Share** buttons, and ornate **Book Again** action bar.
7. **Screen 7: Your feedback builds trust.**  
   Modal popup with folded hands icon 🙏, 5 interactive golden 3D stars ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐, and deep maroon **Submit Rating** button.
8. **Screen 8: PEHLE AAP — PEHLE AAP, PHIR REEL.**  
   Sliding WhatsApp push notification banner, carved royal Rumi Darwaza archway with radiating golden namaste aura, calligraphic logo, and royal slogan.

## 📱 Viewing Modes
- **8-Screen Board (Default):** Displays all 8 phones in their royal arched frames with hanging bells 🔔, corner mandalas, screen numbers, and captions side-by-side.
- **Step-by-Step Simulator:** Interactive full-size phone mockup to walk through the booking flow step by step.
- **Full Platform Portals:** Seamless access to the **Business App**, **Creator App**, **WhatsApp Bridge**, and **Admin & Operations Panel**.
